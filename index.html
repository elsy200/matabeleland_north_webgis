About the Project

This interactive WebGIS presents the spatial distribution of boreholes and their relationship with geological characteristics in Matabeleland North Province, Zimbabwe.

The application allows users to explore borehole locations, borehole depths, geological formations and rivers through an interactive web map.

Users can click on individual features to view their attribute information and use the layer control to switch different map layers on and off.

Study Area: Matabeleland North Province, Zimbabwe
